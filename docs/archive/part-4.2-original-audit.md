## Model dependency and reusable boundaries

`automation.submit()` calls `decision_review.run()` for chart analysis. Without a test provider, review selects the OpenAI client and requires a backend API key. Execution also requires a valid analyst result, reviewer acceptance, fresh image evidence and successful deterministic Python checks. `automation.start()` accepts live/test accounts and explicitly rejects replay execution. The offline SmokeTest injects a controlled provider; passing without credentials there does **not** demonstrate a model-independent production strategy.

Conversely, `sma.sma()`, manual `paper.execute()`, `paper.start_replay()` / `advance_replay()` and P&L calculations operate without model calls. These provide the smallest reusable boundary. Add a separate deterministic strategy orchestration path when requested; preserve the existing AI review gate and do not bypass it or turn its test-provider hook into production trading logic. The independent automatic crossover strategy cannot currently be selected/run in the app.

## Proposed execution assumptions — not implemented by this audit

- Start with existing AAPL, one-minute regular-session completed candles and windows2/3 for explainable verification; support validated choices through existing controls. Missing intervals are not invented; windows span available completed candles. Disclose selection/warm-up boundaries.
- Long-only, one share on an upward crossover when flat and affordable; close the entire position on a downward crossover. No pyramiding, borrowing or shorting. Other states record a reason for no action. This differs from the existing model's configurable quantity and permitted partial sales.
- Start an isolated strategy/backtest account with $1,000. Zero fees/spread/slippage and full fills are simplifying assumptions, not achievable execution claims. Reuse integer-cent fills and exact weighted-average cost basis.
- For receipt-based historical runs, signal only after the completed candle becomes known; queue the order and fill at a strictly later eligible receipt under existing paper rules. Duplicates/identified corrections cannot become new fill events. Recheck cash/holdings at fill. Existing paper fills may use a newly received stale source quote and have no exchange-session execution filter; disclose this explicitly or specify/test a separate strategy eligibility rule before implementation.
- Freeze source selection, receipt cutoff, timeframe/windows, initial cash and policy per run. Do not use wall-clock time or later revisions to finalize past decisions. Keep decision, execution and processing times distinct; never rewrite prior fills after a correction.
- Preserve pending orders and open positions at end of data; do not invent liquidation. Show final valuation time/source and use last usable known completed-candle mark; missing valuation remains unavailable. Reuse the existing documented one-minute mark policy initially, even if chart timeframe differs.
- Compute equity as cash plus marked position value; total return as `(ending equity / initial equity - 1)` and maximum drawdown from the running equity peak, including initial equity. Define valuation sampling; report unavailable metrics when equity is unavailable. No annualization, optimization or benchmark is necessary for the minimum deliverable.
- Prefer recorded Yahoo observations with receipt/revision history. Inspect coverage before claiming a meaningful historical evaluation. If a separate historical bar dataset is needed, record provenance/adjustment/session rules and close-time availability/next-bar fill assumptions; do not claim original receipt history exists. Synthetic fixtures prove arithmetic only.
